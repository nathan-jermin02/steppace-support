Help with StepPace

Support

StepPace is a private, read-only Apple Health companion that helps put today’s steps in the context of a pace you choose.

Contact support

For app issues, feedback, or feature requests, email:

steppaceapp@gmail.com

Include your StepPace version, device model, iOS or watchOS version, and what happened. Please do not include personal Health information.

Requirements

  • iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
  • Apple Watch with watchOS 10 or later for the optional Watch app and complications.
  • Apple Health availability and read permission for live step progress.
  • Optional Walking Speed and Walking Step Length permission for automatic walk-time estimates.

Common questions

My step count is zero or differs from Apple Health

Confirm that Apple Health contains step samples for today. In StepPace, open Settings → Health and choose Request or Refresh Health Access. You can also choose Review Health Access in Settings.

Apple keeps Health read choices private. StepPace cannot distinguish denied read access from a category with no readable samples.

Walking estimates use manual cadence

Automatic cadence requires readable Walking Speed and Walking Step Length samples. Review both rows under Settings → Health and refresh access. If either category is unavailable, StepPace safely uses your manual cadence.

A widget or complication looks stale

Open StepPace and refresh it. Widgets and complications display cached on-device snapshots because they do not query Apple Health directly. Background delivery and widget refresh timing are controlled by the system and are not guaranteed at exact times.

The Apple Watch total is different

The Watch app reads step count directly from Apple Health on the Watch. Confirm Health access for StepPace, open the Watch app, and choose Try Again if an error appears. Allow time for Apple Health to reconcile samples between devices.

Notifications do not arrive

Review notification access, enabled days, active and quiet hours, cooldown, daily maximum, and reminder toggles in StepPace Settings. Then review iOS Settings → Notifications → StepPace. Smart reminders depend on an actual refresh opportunity; a scheduled notification cannot run a Health query when it appears.

How do I remove my StepPace data?

Open StepPace Settings → Privacy Policy & Data and choose Delete Local Progress Data. This removes local history, milestones, notification history, and cached widget progress without changing Apple Health. Deleting the app removes its active local app data; settings retained in an older device backup may require separate removal through Apple’s backup controls.

Privacy and safety

StepPace has no account, advertising, analytics, purchases, subscriptions, tracking, or developer-operated backend. Health access is read-only. Read the full StepPace Privacy Policy.

StepPace is a fitness planning tool, not a medical device, and does not provide medical advice.
