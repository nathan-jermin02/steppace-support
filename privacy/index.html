Privacy Policy

StepPace is operated by Nathan Ryan Jermin in California, United States. It is designed to provide useful step pacing without sending your Health data or app activity to the developer.

Summary

StepPace has no account, developer-operated server, advertising, analytics, or tracking. It does not sell personal information. Health and progress information is processed on your Apple devices and is not accessible to the developer.

Apple Health information

With your permission, StepPace requests read-only access to:

  • Step count, walking speed, and walking step length on iPhone.
  • Step count on Apple Watch.

Step count supports current progress, recent history, pacing, milestones, and movement summaries. Optional walking speed and step length improve on-device cadence, distance, and walk-time estimates. StepPace never writes information to Apple Health.

Health access is optional. Apple does not tell apps whether read access to a particular Health category was denied, so unavailable access can look the same as having no readable samples.

Information stored on your devices

StepPace stores goals, schedules, checkpoints, preferences, onboarding status, daily step-derived summaries, milestones, progress calculations, cached widget and complication snapshots, and a small notification history in app-specific storage.

Daily summary history and cached widget and complication progress snapshots use protected local files that are excluded from device backups. Settings, onboarding, appearance, notification bookkeeping, and Apple Watch configuration remain in local preferences and may be included in backups according to your Apple settings and Apple’s policies. StepPace does not use CloudKit or operate its own cloud synchronization service.

The iPhone app can send settings, estimated cadence, and a derived momentum score to your paired Apple Watch through Apple’s Watch Connectivity service. These transfers remain between your paired Apple devices and are not sent to the developer.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, StepPace creates local reminders on your device. Their content can include locally derived pacing context and may be visible on your Lock Screen according to your notification-preview settings. StepPace does not use a remote push-notification server.

Sharing and third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs and does not transmit app data to the operator, advertisers, data brokers, or analytics providers. Apple supplies operating-system services such as Apple Health, notifications, widgets, Watch Connectivity, backups, TestFlight, and App Store distribution under Apple’s own terms and privacy policies.

This public information site is hosted by GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub may receive ordinary web-request information under the GitHub Privacy Statement. StepPace does not add analytics, advertising, forms, or third-party scripts to this site.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

Local settings and summaries remain on the relevant device while StepPace is installed unless you delete them or the app replaces them during normal operation. There is no server copy.

You can revoke Health or notification access in Apple’s Settings and Health apps. Revoking access stops future reads but does not automatically erase summaries already stored by StepPace.

In StepPace, open Settings → Privacy Policy & Data and choose Delete Local Progress Data to remove local history, milestones, notification history, and cached widget progress. You can also delete StepPace from your iPhone and Apple Watch. Settings or configuration retained in an older device backup may require separate removal through Apple’s backup controls. These actions do not delete Apple Health information because StepPace does not create or own that source data.

Support communications

The app does not automatically transmit support information. If you email support, Nathan Ryan Jermin and the email providers involved receive the address, message, and attachments you choose to send. That information is used to respond, investigate the issue, prevent abuse, and maintain reasonable support records.

Please do not include Health data or other sensitive information in a support message. You may request deletion of eligible support correspondence by emailing steppaceapp@gmail.com.

Children

StepPace is a general-audience fitness planning tool and is not directed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly transmit or collect personal information from children through the app.

Health and medical scope

StepPace is a fitness planning tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition and does not provide medical advice.

California privacy

StepPace does not collect personal information from the app, sell personal information, or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. Because the operator does not receive your in-app Health or progress data, the operator cannot access or return that data.
